Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Graceffo Alert collects, uses, and protects information for recipients of SMS notifications. Graceffo Alert is a personal notification service and is not a commercial marketing platform.

Information Collected

Graceffo Alert collects only the information required to deliver requested SMS notifications, primarily recipient phone numbers and basic delivery status metadata.

Phone Numbers

Phone numbers are only collected from individuals who have explicitly requested notifications from Graceffo Alert.

How Information Is Used

Collected information is used exclusively to send game-entry notifications, provide HELP responses, process STOP opt-outs, and maintain reliable service operations.

Information Sharing

Graceffo Alert does not sell, rent, or share recipient information for marketing, analytics, or advertising. Data is shared only with required SMS delivery infrastructure providers as necessary to transmit messages.

Data Retention

Recipient information is retained only as long as needed to provide requested notifications and maintain operational integrity, then removed when no longer required.

Security

Reasonable technical and operational safeguards are used to protect stored recipient information and service metadata from unauthorized access.

Recipient Rights

Recipients may request updates or deletion of their phone number from the service at any time by using the provided contact method.

Opt-Out

Recipients can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any notification message.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact email@example.com.

No marketing. No analytics. No advertising. No data sales.

Privacy Policy

Graceffo Alert is intentionally designed around minimal data collection and explicit recipient consent.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Graceffo Alert handles information in connection with a personal SMS notification service for Gordon Graceffo game appearances.

The service is non-commercial, invitation-based, and intended only for a fixed list of individuals who requested alerts.

The service is non-commercial and does not offer public registration. Enrollment is limited to personally known individuals who explicitly request notifications directly from the service owner. Recipients are manually added only after providing consent.

Information Collected

The service stores only the phone numbers required to route SMS notifications to approved recipients.

No profile metadata, location history, demographics, browsing behavior, or usage analytics are collected.

Phone Numbers

Phone numbers are collected only after an individual explicitly requests enrollment through the Microsoft Enrollment Request Form. Consent is obtained and all required disclosures are provided before any messages are sent. No phone numbers are purchased, imported, or automatically enrolled.

Phone numbers are used solely to deliver requested informational SMS notifications and to support opt-out handling. Recipients may revoke consent at any time by replying STOP or by contacting the application owner.

Phone numbers are never used for advertising audiences, lookalike targeting, or unrelated outreach.

Purpose of Collection

Phone numbers are collected for one purpose: delivery of informational game appearance alerts to users who asked to receive them.

Consent

Only individuals who explicitly request enrollment are added to the recipient list. Enrollment requests are submitted through the Microsoft Enrollment Request Form. All required disclosures are provided through the form before enrollment is completed.

Consent can be revoked at any time by replying STOP or by submitting an unsubscribe request through the contact channel. Removal is processed promptly.

Enrollment & Consent

Enrollment requests are collected through Microsoft Forms. Information is used solely to evaluate enrollment requests. Phone numbers are collected only after an individual requests enrollment. Recipients are manually approved before receiving any messages. No information is sold or shared. Rejected enrollment requests are not used for marketing or any other purpose.

No phone numbers are purchased, imported, or automatically enrolled from any external source. Recipients may revoke consent at any time by replying STOP or by contacting graceffoalert@outlook.com. See the SMS Program Details page for the complete program information.

Data Storage

Service state and delivery metadata may be persisted in operational storage to support reliability, retries, and campaign recovery.

Data storage is limited to what is required to operate and verify alert delivery behavior.

Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are applied to protect stored phone numbers and operational records from unauthorized access.

Access is restricted to maintainers responsible for running the project.

Retention

Phone numbers are retained only while recipients remain enrolled or until removal is requested.

Operational delivery records may be retained for debugging and reliability review, then removed according to maintenance needs.

Sharing

Recipient phone numbers are not sold, rented, or shared for monetary gain or marketing partnerships.

Third Parties

SMS transport is handled by messaging providers and carriers needed to complete delivery. Those entities process phone numbers as part of message routing.

Graceffo Alert does not authorize third parties to use recipient data for behavioral advertising or independent marketing.

User Rights

Recipients may request confirmation of enrollment status, correction of contact details, or removal from the service.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply START to re-subscribe if still eligible. Reply HELP for support details.

No Cookies or Tracking

The website does not use behavioral tracking, advertising pixels, or marketing cookies.

The service does not run audience analytics for advertising or monetization.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, use the contact channels listed on Contact.

Project Description

Privacy-first, consent-based SMS notifications for approved recipients.
