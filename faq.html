Frequently Asked Questions

What is Graceffo Alert?

Graceffo Alert is a personal notification service that sends SMS updates when Gordon Graceffo enters a St. Louis Cardinals game.

Who receives notifications?

Only individually approved family members and friends who explicitly requested enrollment receive notifications.

How often are messages sent?

Message frequency depends on game appearances and can vary. Most weeks generate zero to five messages.

How do I unsubscribe?

Reply STOP to any message at any time to opt out immediately.

Is this affiliated with MLB?

No. Graceffo Alert is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with MLB or the St. Louis Cardinals organization.

How is my phone number protected?

Phone numbers are used only for requested service alerts and are never sold or used for marketing, analytics, or advertising.

Does it cost anything?

The service is provided as a personal project, but standard carrier SMS/data rates may still apply.

Who built this project?

Graceffo Alert was built and is maintained as a personal software engineering project focused on reliability and observability.
