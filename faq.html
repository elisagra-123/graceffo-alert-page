Frequently Asked Questions

Operational, privacy, and enrollment guidance for Graceffo Alert recipients.

General Questions

1. What is Graceffo Alert?

Graceffo Alert is a personal notification service that tracks official MLB game data and sends an SMS update when Gordon Graceffo enters a St. Louis Cardinals game.

It exists as a software engineering project that demonstrates reliable event detection, campaign orchestration, and observability in a focused real-world use case.

2. Who receives alerts?

Alerts are sent only to a fixed, approved group of family members and friends who explicitly requested enrollment.

The program is not open to unrestricted public sign-up and is intentionally operated at small scale.

3. How do I subscribe?

Subscription requires direct approval from the maintainer and explicit consent to receive informational SMS messages.

If accepted, your number is manually enrolled and can be removed at any time upon request.

4. Can anyone join?

No. Access is invitation-based and intentionally limited to prevent misuse and to keep message volume controlled.

This model also helps maintain strong consent practices and straightforward compliance handling.

5. How often are messages sent?

Messages are event-driven. You may receive no messages during games where Gordon Graceffo does not appear, and one or more messages when he does.

Frequency varies by game conditions, season context, and confirmed in-game participation.

6. Is this affiliated with MLB or the St. Louis Cardinals?

No. Graceffo Alert is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with Major League Baseball, any MLB club, or the St. Louis Cardinals organization.

7. What happens if Gordon Graceffo does not pitch?

No appearance alert is sent. The service only notifies on confirmed in-game entry conditions.

8. How do I unsubscribe?

Reply STOP to unsubscribe immediately from program messages.

You can also request removal through the contact channels listed on the Contact page.

9. How is my phone number protected?

The service stores only the phone number required for delivery. Access is limited to project maintainers responsible for operations.

Data collection is intentionally minimal: no ad profiles, no behavioral analytics, and no resale of recipient data.

10. What messaging provider is used?

Graceffo Alert uses a provider abstraction layer, which means messaging backends can be swapped without redesigning campaign logic.

This architecture supports resilience and helps reduce vendor lock-in risk.

11. What happens if message delivery fails?

Transient failures are handled through bounded retries and campaign state persistence. Delivery outcomes are recorded in a ledger for traceability.

If a provider is degraded, circuit controls and retry policy are designed to keep the system stable while recovery occurs.

12. Is my information shared?

Phone numbers are not sold, rented, or shared for advertising or marketing use.

Necessary processing by messaging providers and carriers occurs only to route SMS traffic.

13. How accurate are alerts?

Alerts are based on official MLB game feeds and detection logic built to confirm true entry events.

As with any live data pipeline, feed delays or provider interruptions can affect timing, and exact delivery guarantees are not possible.

14. Who maintains the service?

Graceffo Alert is maintained as a personal software engineering project focused on reliability, transparency, and practical system design.

15. How can I report an issue?

You can report issues through the repository issue tracker or by contacting the maintainer directly through the Contact page.

When possible, include timing details and message context to help with diagnosis.
