SMS Enrollment

Graceffo Alert is a private, invitation-only SMS notification service. This page documents the complete enrollment process for recipient transparency and independent carrier compliance review.

Only personally known family members and close friends are eligible. The service is not open to the general public. No marketing messages are ever sent. All messages are informational only.

Overview

Graceffo Alert sends one informational SMS notification when MLB pitcher Gordon Graceffo enters a St. Louis Cardinals game. The service is operated for a small, fixed group of fewer than 20 recipients — family members and close friends of the operator who have explicitly requested enrollment.

  • Private, invitation-only service — not open to the public
  • No advertising, no marketing, no fundraising, no purchases
  • Enrollment is limited to personally known individuals
  • Fewer than 20 recipients
  • Explicit consent is obtained before any phone number is added
  • All messages are informational only

How to Request Enrollment

Enrollment follows a deliberate process designed to ensure that every recipient has given explicit consent before receiving any messages.

Step 1 — Review & Request

Eligible individuals review this page and the Request Enrollment page to understand the service, eligibility, and all required disclosures. Eligible individuals then complete the Microsoft Enrollment Request Form.

Step 2 — Manual Review

The application owner reviews every submission individually. Submitting a request does not automatically enroll anyone. Only personally known family members and close friends are eligible.

Step 3 — Consent Confirmed & Manual Approval

Only after the application owner confirms explicit consent has been provided through the enrollment form is the phone number manually added to the approved recipient list. Phone numbers are never purchased, imported, automatically enrolled, shared, or sold.

Step 4 — Receive Alerts

Approved recipients begin receiving informational SMS notifications when Gordon Graceffo enters a St. Louis Cardinals game. Message frequency varies depending on MLB game activity. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe immediately.

Supported SMS Commands

The following standard SMS commands are supported at all times. Recipients may send these keywords in any message.

Supported SMS commands and their descriptions
Command Description
HELP Receive support information and contact details for the service.
STOP Unsubscribe immediately. No further messages will be sent to your number.
START Re-subscribe after opting out, if enrollment remains approved.
Additional supported opt-out keywords

In addition to STOP and START, the following standard carrier opt-out keywords are also recognized: STOPALL, END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT. Any of these keywords will immediately unsubscribe the recipient from all program messages.

Privacy Commitment

Graceffo Alert is committed to protecting the privacy of all recipients.

  • Recipients must explicitly request enrollment before any messages are sent.
  • Phone numbers are manually managed by the application owner.
  • Phone numbers are never sold to third parties.
  • Phone numbers are never shared for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • Recipients may opt out at any time by replying STOP or by emailing graceffoalert@outlook.com.
  • Data collection is limited strictly to the phone number required for delivery.

See the full Privacy Policy for complete details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join?

Graceffo Alert is limited to personally known family members and close friends of the service operator. The service is not open to the general public.

How do I request enrollment?

Complete the Enrollment Request Form by visiting the Request Enrollment page. If your request is approved, your mobile number will be manually added after confirming your consent.

Can anyone sign up?

No. Enrollment is private and invitation-only. The application owner must personally know each recipient before approving any enrollment request.

How often will I receive messages?

Messages are sent only when Gordon Graceffo enters a Cardinals game. Message frequency varies depending on MLB game activity. Some weeks may have no messages.

Can I stop receiving messages?

Yes. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe immediately. You may also email graceffoalert@outlook.com to request removal. Message and data rates may apply.

What information is collected?

Enrollment requests are collected through Microsoft Forms. Information is used solely to evaluate enrollment requests. Phone numbers are collected only after an individual requests enrollment and is approved. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

Is my phone number shared or sold?

No. Phone numbers are never sold, shared, or used for any purpose other than delivering the informational SMS notifications you explicitly requested.
