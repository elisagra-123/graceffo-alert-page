Enrollment Request Received

Your enrollment request has been received. The application owner will review your request.

Submitting the form does not automatically enroll you. If approved, your mobile number will be manually added to the Graceffo Alert notification list. Only approved recipients receive notifications.

What Happens Next

  1. 1
    Enrollment Request Submitted

    Your Enrollment Request Form has been received by Graceffo Alert.

  2. 2
    Manual Review

    The application owner reviews your request to verify eligibility. Every request is reviewed individually.

  3. 3
    Consent Confirmed

    The application owner confirms that explicit consent was provided through the enrollment form before proceeding.

  4. 4
    Manual Approval

    If approved, your phone number is manually added to the notification list. No phone number is ever automatically enrolled.

  5. 5
    SMS Notifications Begin

    Approved recipients receive informational SMS notifications when Gordon Graceffo enters a St. Louis Cardinals game.

Important Notes

  • Submitting this form does not enroll you in Graceffo Alert.
  • You will not receive SMS messages unless your request is manually approved.
  • Enrollment is limited to personally known family members and close friends.
  • Approval is not guaranteed.

SMS Program Disclosures

As noted in the Enrollment Request Form, all recipients receive the following disclosures before any messages are sent:

  • ✓ Informational messages only
  • ✓ Message frequency varies
  • ✓ Message & Data Rates May Apply
  • ✓ Reply STOP to unsubscribe
  • ✓ Reply HELP for assistance
  • ✓ Manual approval required
  • ✓ Consent is not required to purchase goods or services
  • ✓ No marketing
  • ✓ No advertising
  • ✓ No fundraising

Questions?

For questions about your enrollment request or the review process, contact graceffoalert@outlook.com.

If you wish to withdraw your request at any time, contact graceffoalert@outlook.com.
