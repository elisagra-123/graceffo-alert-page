Terms & Conditions

Graceffo Alert provides informational SMS notifications for approved recipients on an availability basis.

SMS Messaging Terms

Graceffo Alert is an informational SMS program. The following terms apply to all program messages.

  • Message type: Informational notifications only. No marketing or promotional messages are sent. No advertising. No fundraising.
  • Enrollment: Submitting an enrollment request does not create enrollment. Enrollment begins only after manual approval by the application owner. Each prospective recipient must complete the Enrollment Request Form. Phone numbers are manually added only after explicit consent is confirmed through the enrollment form.
  • Message frequency: Message frequency varies based on MLB game activity. Messages are event-triggered, not scheduled. Some weeks may have no messages.
  • Opt out: Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe immediately. Additional supported opt-out keywords: STOPALL, END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT.
  • Help: Reply HELP for support information and contact details.
  • Rates: Message and data rates may apply according to each recipient's mobile carrier plan.
  • Eligibility: The service is limited to personally known family members and close friends of the operator. The service is not open to the general public.
  • Removal: Recipients may be removed upon request at any time. Reply STOP or contact the application owner directly.
  • Consent: Consent is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

See the SMS Enrollment page and the Request Enrollment page for the complete enrollment and consent workflow.

Service Description

Graceffo Alert monitors official MLB game data and sends informational SMS messages when Gordon Graceffo enters a St. Louis Cardinals game.

Eligibility

The service does not offer public registration. Enrollment is limited to personally known individuals who explicitly request notifications directly from the service owner. Recipients are manually added only after providing consent.

Availability

The service is maintained as a personal project and may be modified, paused, or discontinued at any time.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on game activity and may be irregular. Alerts are event-triggered rather than scheduled.

Carrier Charges

Message and data rates may apply according to each recipient's carrier plan.

Accuracy

Alerts depend on the timing and correctness of official MLB data feeds and downstream messaging infrastructure.

While the system is engineered for reliability, absolute real-time precision and perfect completeness cannot be guaranteed.

Temporary interruptions may occur due to network conditions, provider outages, data feed delays, or maintenance activity.
